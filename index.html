CLEAR PROMISES · HONEST LIMITS

Privacy centre.

Understand what CLASSVOICE is designed to protect, what it stores, and what it cannot promise.

◎

No names on concerns

Published concerns and reaction totals must not display a student's name, email, roll number or voter identity.

⌁

No account or email required

CLASSVOICE will not ask for college email or OTP. Without individual verification, however, it cannot prove that every visitor is an ET-D student.

◌

One browser, one reaction (demo)

The demo remembers reactions on this browser only. Clearing browser data or switching devices can reset this limit.

▤

Aggregate reports only

CR Sarthak Jain and ACR Prashashti Wankhede are intended report viewers, not identity viewers. Small groups must be suppressed.

!
Important: no-login means no verified class membership

This prototype does not ask for college email, OTP, name or student account. It also has no shared database. A future public backend can still process technical metadata such as IP address and timestamps. Do not promise absolute untraceability or submit real/sensitive complaints until the backend and privacy controls are independently reviewed.
